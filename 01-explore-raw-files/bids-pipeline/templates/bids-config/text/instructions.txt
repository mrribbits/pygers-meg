You will hear a series of tones.

On each trial an arrow cue appears:
    <  <   = LEFT hand          >  >   = RIGHT hand

After the cue you will hear several identical tones,
followed by ONE different (higher) tone.

As soon as you hear the DIFFERENT tone,
press the INDEX FINGER button on the cued hand
as quickly as possible.

If you miss or press the wrong button, you'll see feedback.

When 'BLINK' appears, you have half a second to blink.
Otherwise please try not to blink and hold your gaze on the cross.
