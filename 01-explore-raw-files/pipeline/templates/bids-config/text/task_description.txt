Active cued auditory oddball paradigm pulled from FieldTrip's tutorial (https://www.fieldtriptoolbox.org/workshop/natmeg2014/meg_audodd/). 3 blocks × 100 trials (~51 minutes), continuous EyeLink recording, VPIXX pixel-mode triggers, and hardware-scheduled VPIXX audio. The subject sees an arrow cue directing them to prepare a left- or right-hand response, hears 3–7 standard tones (1000 Hz) followed by one deviant tone (1200 Hz), and presses the index-finger button on the cued hand as quickly as possible. Supports classical MMN/P3 analysis on tone-locked ERFs and lateralized post-movement beta rebound (PMBR) analysis on the cued-hand contrast. The PsychoPy code is available here: https://github.com/mrribbits/Auditory-Oddball-Psychopy
